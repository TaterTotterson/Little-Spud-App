# Google Play listing (English — United States)

## App name

Little Spud

## Short description

Your self-hosted Tater companion for chat, smart home control, and music.

## Full description

Little Spud is the Android companion for Tater, a user-controlled, self-hosted assistant.

Pair Little Spud with your own Tater instance using a QR code, then keep its most useful features close at hand.

CHAT WITH TATER

• Send messages using text or voice input
• Attach images and view media replies
• Keep recent conversations available on your device

CONTROL YOUR HOME

• See rooms, device status, and temperature at a glance
• Control supported lights, switches, and thermostats
• View supported camera snapshots
• Choose Fahrenheit or Celsius

ENJOY YOUR MUSIC

• Search and browse your Music Core library
• Explore artists, albums, genres, and recommendations
• Choose local or remote playback targets
• Control playback, queue, and volume

STAY INFORMED

• Receive private notifications from your paired Tater
• Review recent alerts inside the app

SELF-HOSTED BY DESIGN

Little Spud does not provide a hosted account or public Tater server. Connected features require a compatible Tater instance operated and configured by the user. Demo Mode lets you explore sample chat, Home, and Music experiences without a server.

Little Spud is free and contains no ads, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or paid feature unlocks.

Learn more about Tater at https://taterassistant.com.
